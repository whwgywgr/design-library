Web design is dead (again). We disagree.

Posted 12 Oct 2006 by admin · Category: Web Design · 32 comments · Permalink

🖥️Every few months somebody declares web design finished. First tables were going to kill it, then Flash, then "the industry" moved on to gradients, rounded corners and reflections on everything.

Here at PixelPortal we say: good design never dies, it just changes its hairstyle. Remember when a drop shadow had to be a sliced GIF? When rounded corners were four images and a prayer? We do — and honestly, we kind of miss the craftsmanship.

This week we look back at five techniques from the golden era that still hold up: the humble breadcrumb, the trusty left sidebar, the gloss button you can almost feel, the 1px bevel, and the word "community" used without irony.

Agree? Disagree? The comment box is right below, the guestbook is still warm, and the forum is open 24 hours. The interwebs is what we make it, folks. — The Editor
